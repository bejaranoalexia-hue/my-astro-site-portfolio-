## What this is for

Creative work fails quietly when the routing behind it is wrong. Leads land in the wrong inbox, sources get renamed in three places, and the report no longer matches the CRM. Marketing operations here means making those definitions one thing.

## How it usually runs

Three to five weeks. First an inventory of every source, then a routing map the sales team will actually follow, then tracking that uses the same names.

## What you get

Mapped sources, live routing, and a report that does not contradict the CRM.
