Search behavior has changed. Prospective clients still use Google, but they increasingly ask full, conversational questions to Perplexity, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. My SEO and AEO (answer engine optimization) services combine technical site health, local search, and entity-based optimization with content structured for AI answers, so your business shows up in both the search results and the answers people now read first.

Based in Dallas, Texas, I work fully remotely with mid-size companies across the US and Latin America, including home service businesses and B2B software companies.

**Works with:** Google Search Console, Google Business Profile, SEMrush, Google Analytics

## Why SEO and AEO Matter Together

Organic visibility now depends on two audiences: search crawlers and AI answer engines. A strong strategy serves both.

- **Conversational and long-tail queries.** Short searches tend to trigger map listings and directories, while longer, problem-solving questions trigger AI Overviews and chatbot answers. Clear, authoritative, value-first content gives you a chance to be cited in them.
- **Local search visibility.** High-intent customers search by location. Localized page structure, consistent business listings, and a well-built Google Business Profile help you appear where they look.
- **Technical site health.** Crawl errors, orphan pages, and duplicate titles limit how well search engines can read your site. Fixing them improves crawl efficiency and indexing.
- **No keyword cannibalization.** Clean title tags, URL structure, and page targeting make sure your own pages don't compete against each other for the same terms.
- **Off-page authority.** AI engines weigh what the web says about you. Consistent citations, relevant partnerships, and an active presence across trusted platforms give them the verification they look for.

## How It Works

### 1. Technical Health Audit

I review crawlability, indexation, and site hygiene, then fix broken links, orphan pages, duplicate titles, and any harmful backlinks that need action.

**Typical tools:** Google Search Console, SEMrush

### 2. Entity Mapping and On-Page Optimization

Using keyword gap analysis, I organize your content hierarchy and refine titles, H1s, meta descriptions, and readability. The focus is on long-tail terms that support your primary keywords without competing with them.

### 3. Local SEO and Citation Setup

I build consistent business listings, sync them with your Google Business Profile, and create location or service-area pages where they make sense for your business.

**Typical tools:** Google Business Profile, trusted business directories

### 4. AEO Content Hubs

I create structured, question-and-answer content that answers the full set of related questions an AI engine explores when responding to a complex query (often called query fan-out), so your brand is a strong candidate for citation in Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI Overviews.
